# Palora

**Thoughtful, personalized handmade gifts that turn simple moments into lasting memories.**

## 📖 About Palora

Palora is a boutique handmade gifting business born out of a passion for craftsmanship and personal connection. In a world of mass-produced goods, Palora focuses on creating unique, artisanal items that carry genuine emotional weight. 

Whether it is for a birthday, an anniversary, a festival, or a "just because" surprise, Palora provides customers with high-quality, customized gifts that make the recipient feel truly special.

## 🎁 What We Make

Palora specializes in a wide variety of handcrafted and highly customizable products, categorized to help you find the perfect gift for every occasion:

* **Occasions & Celebrations:** Birthdays, Anniversaries, Festivals, and "Just Because" moments.
* **Handmade Crafts:** Intricate crochet items, custom resin coasters, and designed frames.
* **Floral & Decor:** Beautiful bouquets, flowers, and unique home decor pieces.
* **Personalized Touches:** Custom keychains, handcrafted letters, bespoke cards, and curated gift hampers.

## ✨ Platform Key Features

This digital storefront was custom-engineered to provide a warm, frictionless shopping experience for Palora's customers. 

* **📱 Mobile-First Architecture:** A fluid, touch-friendly UI designed specifically for shoppers discovering Palora through social media on their smartphones.
* **🛍️ Frictionless Browsing:** No forced account creation or login required. Customers can browse, select, and purchase immediately.
* **✍️ Item-Level Personalization:** Dedicated text inputs on every product card allow customers to add names, dates, or special custom notes easily.
* **💬 Direct WhatsApp Checkout:** A serverless checkout flow that compiles the user's entire cart, total pricing, and personalization notes into a structured message, seamlessly redirecting the buyer straight to Palora's official WhatsApp to finalize the order.
* **⚡ Lightning Fast:** Built with lightweight Vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, ensuring instant load times and no heavy backend delays.
