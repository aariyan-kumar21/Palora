    How to order

    1. 01

      Choose the pieces you love and add them to your cart.

    2. 02

      Add your personalization notes, such as a name, message or colour preference.

    3. 03

      Send your order on WhatsApp. We confirm the details, delivery time and payment with you before we begin.

    Personalization

    Many of our pieces can be personalized with names, messages or colour preferences. Look for the Personalizable badge, or add a note on any product in its quick view. If you would like something more custom, tell us on WhatsApp.

    Shipping & delivery

    We deliver across India. Every piece is made to order, so delivery timing depends on the piece and any personalization. We confirm the timing with you on WhatsApp when you place your order.

    Returns & personalized orders

    Because each piece is made to order, please check your order details carefully before confirming. If your order arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, message us on WhatsApp with photos and we will find the best solution together.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I place an order?

    Add your pieces to the cart, include your personalization notes, and send the order to us on WhatsApp. We confirm everything with you.

    Can I personalize any product?

    Many pieces can be personalized. Products with the Personalizable badge are made for it, and you can add a note to any product. If something is not possible, we will tell you on WhatsApp.

    Do you deliver across India?

    Yes, we deliver across India.

    How long will my order take?

    Every piece is made to order, so timing depends on the piece and its personalization. We confirm delivery timing with you on WhatsApp when you order.

    How do I pay?

    We share the payment details with you on WhatsApp when we confirm your order.

    Can I change or cancel my order?

    Message us on WhatsApp as soon as possible. Because every piece is made to order, changes are easiest before we begin making it.

    Can I order in bulk or ask for custom colours?

    Yes. Tell us what you have in mind on WhatsApp and we will see what we can do for you.
